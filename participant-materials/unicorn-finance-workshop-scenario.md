# Unicorn Finance Philippines — workshop scenario

**Workshop date:** 22 September 2026
**Audience:** Home Credit Philippines
**Scenario:** Fictional company and synthetic data

## Your role today

You are joining the internal data team at **Unicorn Finance Philippines**, a fictional consumer lender operating through retail partners and digital channels.

An implementation partner, **Atlas Ridge Consulting**, has delivered Unicorn Finance's Databricks lakehouse and is handing it over to your team. The platform contains useful data, notebooks, dashboards, metrics, pipelines, models, and AI assets—but operating them is now Unicorn Finance's responsibility.

Your job is not simply to run prepared code. By the end of the workshop, your team should be able to explain:

- what each inherited asset is for;
- which business definition it depends on;
- which compute and permissions it requires;
- how to validate its results;
- where to monitor it;
- how to recover it when something fails;
- who owns its next change.

## The business event

**Nova Mobile**, a fictional smartphone brand, has funded a limited retail financing campaign. Approved customers buying selected Nova smartphones at participating stores can repay the financed principal over 6, 9, or 12 monthly installments at **0% monthly interest**. Nova Mobile pays Unicorn Finance a brand subsidy for contracts originated through the campaign.

“0%” refers only to the contractual interest rate. The phone is not free: customers still repay the financed principal and may pay an applicable processing fee. The offer does not imply zero down payment, zero fees, or guaranteed approval. In the workshop data, the campaign is identified by promotion code `ZERO_SMARTPHONE_2026` and labeled **0% smartphone promotion**.

Application and approval volumes increased during the campaign, but first-payment-default behavior appears concentrated in a small number of stores and sales associates.

This pattern is an **investigation signal**. It is not proof of fraud, misconduct, or causality. The pattern could reflect campaign design, customer mix, origination controls, operational behavior, or another explanation.

The workshop follows this question through the entire Databricks platform:

> What happened during the promotion, which evidence can we trust, and what must Unicorn Finance operate after the partner handover?

## The inherited data platform

The synthetic lending data is stored in Unity Catalog:

```text
hc_workshop
├── core_lending      protected source data
├── workshop_shared   trusted views, metrics, and facilitator-managed assets
└── workshop_labs     participant-owned tables and models
```

The eight `core_lending` tables describe one connected lending lifecycle:

```text
customer → loan_application → credit_contract → installment → payment
                    │                            └──→ collection_action
                    ├→ loan_product
                    └→ retail_location
```

## The shared business definition

For this workshop, **FPD5** means that the first installment reached five days past due before full settlement.

Include only contracts whose first installment has reached that observation point by the declared as-of date, **2026-09-01**. A null settlement date, or settlement on or after day five, counts as FPD5. The denominator is eligible contracts—not every application or approval.

Every section must preserve this definition rather than creating a competing version.

## What we are delivering for Unicorn Finance

### Introduction to Databricks — understand the inherited platform

**Business need:** The internal team needs a map of the platform it now owns.

**Delivery:** A shared understanding of the Databricks workspace, Unity Catalog, compute surfaces, and the relationship between data assets and workspace assets.

### Data Analysis in Databricks — deliver a trusted investigation

**Business need:** Risk and operations teams need evidence about Nova Mobile's 0%-interest financing campaign without overstating what the pattern proves.

**Delivery:**

- a reproducible FPD5 cohort and hotspot analysis;
- a participant-owned Delta investigation table with history;
- a governed Metric View containing the shared FPD5 measures;
- an AI/BI Dashboard that reconciles to the analysis;
- a private Genie Agent with a verified baseline answer;
- an operational view of the SQL workload generated by those assets.

### Generative AI in Databricks — accelerate the work without weakening trust

**Business need:** Analysts and engineers need to understand and improve inherited work faster while retaining review and validation.

**Delivery:**

- an explained and extended regional FPD5 analysis;
- a repaired PySpark denominator calculation with executable checks;
- documented, reviewed, result-preserving improvements;
- a validated, unpublished dashboard draft created with Genie Code;
- optionally, a small regression-tested improvement to the private Genie Agent.

### Data Engineering in Databricks — make the logic repeatable

**Business need:** A one-time investigation is not enough; selected logic must run reliably as an operated workload.

**Delivery:** An orchestrated data workflow using Jobs and Lakeflow capabilities, with explicit data-quality behavior, monitoring, and recovery responsibilities.

### Governance and Access Control — deliver a safe operating model

**Business need:** The internal team must diagnose access failures without applying unnecessarily broad permissions, and consumers must be able to discover trusted assets.

**Delivery:**

- a reusable access-diagnostic sequence;
- evidence showing the difference between workspace access, runtime identity, and Unity Catalog privileges;
- the narrowest repair for a controlled missing-privilege scenario;
- a lineage-based view of downstream impact;
- a Consumer Lending discovery structure that organizes trusted assets without granting access;
- a completed incident handover recording evidence, verification, owner, risk, and recovery.

This is a facilitator-led demonstration. Participants follow one incident from discovery through diagnosis, repair, verification, lineage, and handover using a single participant guide. They do not run code or change permissions.

### Machine Learning in Databricks — deliver an operable risk model

**Business need:** The team wants a governed, interpretable way to rank eligible contracts for further investigation.

**Delivery:**

- a leakage-safe, time-split FPD5 classifier;
- an MLflow run with parameters, metrics, and model artifacts;
- a registered Unity Catalog model with a champion alias and lineage;
- a governed batch-scoring output;
- a handover decision covering ownership, retraining, monitoring, promotion, and rollback.

## The handover standard

At the end of the day, every asset should have:

1. a business purpose;
2. a named owner;
3. a governed source and definition;
4. an appropriate compute dependency;
5. explicit permissions;
6. a validation check;
7. a monitoring location;
8. a recovery or rollback action.

That is the outcome of the workshop: not merely a set of notebooks, but a platform that Unicorn Finance's internal team can understand, trust, and operate.
