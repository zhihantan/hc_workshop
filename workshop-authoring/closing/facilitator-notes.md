# Facilitator notes — Unicorn Finance workshop close

## Purpose

Use the close to complete the story introduced in the morning. The audience should leave understanding how the day changed Unicorn Finance's position:

```text
Inherited assets + unresolved signal
                ↓
Trusted evidence + repeatable workloads + governed access + operable model
                ↓
Named internal ownership
```

Do not present a chronological feature recap. Anchor every statement in the 0% promotion, shared FPD5 definition, partner handover, and operating responsibilities.

## Recommended 8–10 minute run

- **0:00–1:00 — Slide 1:** Restate the starting condition.
- **1:00–2:00 — Slide 2:** Connect the sessions through one business question.
- **2:00–3:30 — Slide 3:** Summarize the delivered operating path.
- **3:30–4:30 — Slide 4:** Show the explicit before-and-after transformation.
- **4:30–5:30 — Slide 5:** Reinforce the reusable control standard.
- **5:30–6:30 — Slide 6:** Translate artifacts into internal responsibilities.
- **6:30–8:30 — Slide 7:** Run the handover reflection and take two responses.
- **8:30–9:00 — Optional Slide 8:** Close the narrative.

## Day-of validation

Before presenting the closing slides, mark each activity as:

- **Completed by participants**
- **Facilitator demonstrated**
- **Discussed only**
- **Skipped**

Use those exact distinctions in presenter language. Do not say “you built” when participants only watched a demonstration.

Confirm these points:

- Was the FPD5 cohort calculated and reconciled?
- Was a participant Delta result written and its history inspected?
- Was the Metric View used in the dashboard and Genie Agent?
- Was the Genie Code denominator bug repaired and validated?
- Which Data Engineering workload was actually run?
- Was the controlled governance failure reproduced and repaired?
- Was lineage or the Consumer Lending discovery structure shown?
- Was the MLflow run completed?
- Was the model registered and batch scoring written?
- Did participants complete an ownership or handover note?

If an expected activity was skipped, retain the business outcome but change the wording:

- “You built” → “We examined how to build”
- “You validated” → “We defined the validation required”
- “You operated” → “We identified the operating controls”
- “We delivered” → “The target delivery is”

## Optional live-result callouts

Use actual values only after validating them in the workspace. Do not add invented participant results to the deck.

Potential callouts:

- promotion FPD5 rate compared with the eligible comparison cohort;
- one store, region, or associate concentration investigated by participants;
- one code defect found and the assertion that now catches it;
- one pipeline quality rule and its configured behavior;
- the exact missing Unity Catalog privilege in the controlled demonstration;
- one model evaluation metric and one leakage feature deliberately excluded;
- one concrete owner, monitor, or recovery action from the closing reflection.

Prefer two verified callouts over a dense scorecard.

## Closing questions

If time permits, ask:

1. Which asset would be riskiest to inherit without documentation?
2. Which validation should block a release?
3. Which access failure would your team currently struggle to diagnose?
4. Who decides whether a model becomes the new champion?
5. Where would an operator look first when tomorrow's run fails?

## Required guardrails

- Unicorn Finance and Atlas Ridge Consulting are fictional.
- All records and workshop outcomes are synthetic.
- The FPD5 pattern is an investigation signal, not proof of fraud or causality.
- A dashboard, Genie answer, or model prediction is not trustworthy merely because it executes successfully.
- Discover Domains organize assets for discovery; they do not grant access.
- The governance notebook is facilitator-run under a restricted identity.
- Machine-learning scores support investigation and prioritization; they do not establish misconduct.
- Do not claim that workshop artifacts are production-ready without the missing production controls and approvals.

## Final line

Use this line or a close equivalent:

> Atlas Ridge delivered the starting platform. The outcome of today is that Unicorn Finance now has a method for trusting, governing, operating, recovering, and improving what it owns.
